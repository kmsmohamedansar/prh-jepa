# Claims ledger

Every claim in `paper/report.md` appears here with the numbers behind it, where they come from, and how it was verified. Numbers come from `paper/numbers.json`, which `.venv/bin/python scripts/report_numbers.py` rebuilds from `results/phase1.json`, `results/phase2_wit1024.json`, `results/phase3_views.json` and `results/raw/extract-*.json`.

How verified: **script** = produced by an executable script in `scripts/` and saved in `results/`. **read** = inspected directly in the pinned upstream source or licence file. **both** = checked by script and inspected by eye.

All runs: one Mac (`macOS-27.0.1-arm64-arm-64bit`; Apple M4, 16 GB unified memory, reported by the owner), Python `3.12.15`, PyTorch `2.15.0`, `transformers` `5.22.1`, `timm` `1.0.31`, `datasets` `5.1.0`, `numpy` `2.5.4`, testing `galilai-group/prh-proof` at commit `a2b9949fa907d4fbe562742d4b1ffa7205ab7427` with `galilai-group/stable-pretraining` at `ab836bf699a2be712dfcf980a5eb70d36391e876`.

## What this report does not claim

- It does **not** verify or refute the authors' 940M-image JEPA runs (`0.184` on WIT-1024), 72B LLM baselines, pair-free alignment numbers, or video/image captioning scores beyond checking that their numbers rebuild from `paper/data/*.csv` (with `tab:captioner_scale` noted as typed).
- It does **not** claim that laptop-scale `BERT-T` training (`51,200` scenes) tests the asymptotic regime of the paper's 5,500 GPU-hour runs.
- It does **not** claim that Theorems 1 to 4 hold outside their stated assumptions (finite or compact operator setting, exact global minimisers, matched world-state marginals across modalities, and conditionally independent nuisances for exact alignment).
